Cost of Living Crisis: Grocery Prices Up 11% Year-on-Year

Households are cutting back on essentials as supermarket profits soar

Site Administrator Monday, 28 September 2026 815 views
Share:

New figures from the Australian Bureau of Statistics show that grocery prices have risen 11.2% over the past twelve months, squeezing family budgets at a time when wage growth has averaged just 4.1%.

The data, compiled from a basket of 200 common household goods, reveals that staples like bread (+14%), dairy (+18%), and fresh vegetables (+22%) have recorded the steepest price rises.

Supermarket Profits Under Scrutiny

The price surge comes as both Woolworths and Coles posted record after-tax profits in their most recent annual reports, prompting renewed calls from consumer advocates for a federal inquiry into supermarket pricing practices.

"Australians cannot afford to eat properly while shareholders pocket billions," said Choice CEO Alan Kirkland. "An independent watchdog with real teeth is long overdue."

Government Response

The Prime Minister has pledged to introduce a mandatory grocery price reporting code before the end of the parliamentary year, though opposition figures have dismissed the move as insufficient.
