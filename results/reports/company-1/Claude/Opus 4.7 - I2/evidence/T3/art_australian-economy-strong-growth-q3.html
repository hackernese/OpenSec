Australian Economy Shows Strong Growth in Q3

GDP expands by 2.1% as employment reaches record highs

Site Administrator Saturday, 03 October 2026 159 views
Share:

Australia's economy demonstrated remarkable resilience in the third quarter, with GDP expanding by 2.1% — exceeding analyst expectations of 1.7%.

The Reserve Bank of Australia attributed the growth to strong consumer spending, robust exports, and a recovering housing market.

Key Highlights

  • GDP growth: 2.1% (forecast: 1.7%)
  • Unemployment rate: 3.8% (lowest in 15 years)
  • Consumer confidence index: 108.3
  • Exports up 4.2% year-on-year

Treasurer commented that the figures demonstrate Australia's economic fundamentals remain strong despite global headwinds.
