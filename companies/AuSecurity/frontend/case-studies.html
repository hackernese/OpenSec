Engagements Across Industries

Every engagement is unique — here are four examples that illustrate our depth of capability.

Financial Services · GRC

ISO 27001 Certification Achieved in 9 Months

Challenge A regional lending institution needed ISO 27001 certification to satisfy enterprise client procurement requirements, but had no existing ISMS documentation and limited internal security capability.
Approach Ausecurity conducted an initial gap assessment, developed a complete ISMS policy suite, delivered staff awareness training, and provided hands-on support through the internal audit and certification audit process.
✅ Achieved ISO 27001:2022 certification within 9 months. Zero non-conformities raised during certification audit.
E-Commerce · Penetration Testing

Critical SQLi Vulnerability Discovered Pre-Launch

Challenge An e-commerce platform was six weeks from launch and required an independent security assessment before going live. The platform handled payment card data and customer PII.
Approach A full web application penetration test was conducted against the staging environment, including manual testing of authentication flows, checkout logic, admin interfaces, and API endpoints.
✅ Discovered and documented a critical SQL injection vulnerability in the order history endpoint. Fully remediated before go-live — protecting 50,000+ customer records.
Healthcare · GRC + Training

Full GRC Uplift & Security Awareness Programme

Challenge A multi-site healthcare provider had grown rapidly through acquisitions and had fragmented, inconsistent security practices across its locations. Staff were regularly falling for phishing simulations.
Approach A 12-month vCISO engagement was established. Ausecurity consolidated the risk register, standardised policy documentation, conducted a full NIST CSF assessment, and delivered quarterly phishing simulations with targeted training.
✅ Phishing click rate reduced from 34% to 6% over 12 months. Organisation achieved NIST CSF Tier 3 posture from a Tier 1 baseline.
Government Contractor · Red Team

Red Team Assessment Reveals Critical AD Misconfigurations

Challenge A government contractor handling sensitive data required an adversarial simulation to validate the effectiveness of their recently upgraded SOC and endpoint detection capabilities.
Approach A goal-based red team exercise was scoped to simulate an advanced persistent threat actor. The team gained initial access via a phishing email, escalated privileges through an unconstrained delegation misconfiguration in Active Directory, and achieved domain compromise.
✅ Domain compromise achieved in 4 days without triggering any SOC alerts. Full MITRE ATT&CK-mapped report delivered. AD hardening project initiated — 17 misconfigurations resolved.
